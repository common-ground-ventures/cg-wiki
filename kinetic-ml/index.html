Wiki/Kinetic ML

Overview

Kinetic ML is a concept-stage business-intelligence platform. Its working line: “See everything about any business. In seconds.” The system scrapes a business’s website, identifies the owner or decision maker, surfaces social profiles and contact details, and generates personalized outreach from what it finds.

The current feature set covers website intelligence (headlines, services, tech stack), decision-maker discovery, and personalized outreach generation, with a live demo on the public site. Common Ground advises Kinetic ML; the relationship dates to 2025. The venture is at concept stage and this page will be updated as it matures.
