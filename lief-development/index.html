Overview

Lïef Development + Construction is a luxury residential developer and builder based in Arizona, positioned as “Arizona’s Premier Luxury Home Builder.” The firm designs and builds high-end custom homes, and through LÏEF Builders provides owner's representation, architecture, preconstruction and general contracting nationally. Its estimating practice runs on Kanopi, the engine the firm built for its own bids.

Lïef is led by Jesse Fowler, Common Ground’s Execution & Development principal, whose prior firm, Tellus Design + Build, ran from 2004 to 2023 and completed more than 400 projects and over $300M of built work with architecture, estimating and construction under one roof. Lïef has been a principal holding in the Common Ground portfolio since 2023 and anchors the group’s real estate practice.
