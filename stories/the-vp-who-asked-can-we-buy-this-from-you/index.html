The VP who asked, 'Can we buy this from you?'

Wiki/Stories/The VP who asked, 'Can we buy this from you?'

A building materials manufacturer's VP of Operations ran three same day blind takeoffs against Kanopi to see whether it could match his own team's internal estimating process. Instead of polishing the results before sending them, my team ran a blind second pass on each takeoff first, which caught real errors, a shell height mistake, an undercounted window schedule, a slab thickness conflict, before anything went out the door. A trial like this only means something if it survives someone else checking your work. Catching your own mistakes before the manufacturer's estimator does is the entire difference between a demo and a credible product. Each corrected takeoff took under an hour. The VP's first reaction was pretty damn close, I'm sold, his own team's check came within a couple thousand board feet and twenty to thirty bags of my numbers, and when he floated buying the capability outright I told him the truth, the IP is expensive. The trial that actually wins someone over is the one where you catch your own mistakes before they do. I didn't let the pressure of a live trial push a rushed answer out the door. I built in the discipline to catch my own errors first.
