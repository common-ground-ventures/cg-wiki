Overview

Bridgepoint Aviation, operating publicly as BridgePoint Air Advisors, is a private aviation advisory firm. Its line is “Private aviation, professionally managed”: one dedicated advisor coordinating across charter, jet-card, fractional, and owned flights, with no handoffs.

Services include aviation consulting, contract negotiations, charter flight sourcing, portfolio optimization across ownership and charter models, and trip coordination. The firm’s stated case results include 41% cost savings achieved by leveraging open-market opportunities and 23% optimization beyond fractional ownership structures.

Common Ground advises Bridgepoint Aviation; the relationship dates to 2025.
