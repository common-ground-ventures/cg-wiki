Wiki/Xtrata

Overview

Xtrata (formerly operating as Strata) is a construction technology company based in Tempe, Arizona. It builds with SABS, the Saebi Alternative Building System: a patented three-layer composite technology that combines EPS foam panels with a high-strength concrete composite coating, replacing traditional wood framing.

Published performance figures for the system include a 0% flame spread rating, resistance to 250 mph hurricane and tornado winds, insulation values of R-75 to R-100, a Sound Transmission Class of 52, and 100% recyclable materials, with average cost savings of 15 to 20% and comparable gains in build speed. The company’s services span residential and commercial construction, post-disaster building, ADUs, design and structural engineering, and SABS certification training for contractors.

Common Ground advises Xtrata on brand; the relationship dates to 2025.
