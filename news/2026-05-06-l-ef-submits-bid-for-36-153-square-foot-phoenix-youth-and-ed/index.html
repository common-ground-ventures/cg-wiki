Lïef Submits Bid for 36,153-Square-Foot Phoenix Youth and Education Campus

News · May 6, 2026

Posted September 2026. Covers May 6, 2026.

Posted September 2026 · covers May 6, 2026

PHOENIX, AZ, May 6, 2026. Lïef has submitted a bid for a 36,153-square-foot youth and education campus in Phoenix, covering assembly, classroom, and activity space for a faith-based youth organization.

The engineering story is the differentiator: the facility was re-engineered as SABS, the proprietary structural block system, in lieu of the pre-engineered metal building the project was originally conceived around, with self-performed labor on footings, slab, and the block package. The switch trades commodity steel for a wall system with superior thermal and fire performance at a competitive number.

The estimate was produced through Kanopi with the platform's standard blind second pass.
