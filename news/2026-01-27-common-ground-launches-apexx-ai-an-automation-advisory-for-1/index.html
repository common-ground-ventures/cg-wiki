Common Ground Launches APEXX AI, an Automation Advisory for $1M to $10M Businesses

News · January 27, 2026

Posted September 2026. Covers January 27, 2026.

Posted September 2026 · covers January 27, 2026

PHOENIX, AZ, January 27, 2026. Common Ground today announced APEXX AI, an advisory practice focused on a segment the AI wave keeps skipping: businesses doing $1M to $10M in revenue that are too big to run on sticky notes and too small for enterprise software.

APEXX AI engagements cover three layers. First, front-of-house automation: AI phone reception and booking that answers every call. Second, visibility: structured local-search and AI-answer-engine presence so the business is found and cited when customers ask a machine for a recommendation. Third, back office: workflow automation across scheduling, follow-up, and reporting.

The practice operates advisor-first. Owners keep their tools and their team; APEXX AI maps what should be automated, in what order, and what it is worth before anything gets built.
