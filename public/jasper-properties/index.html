Overview

Jasper Properties is a boutique Airbnb property management company serving Phoenix, Arizona, with a service area concentrated around Arcadia, Biltmore, Paradise Valley, and Scottsdale. The firm handles guest communication, cleaning coordination, maintenance, and pricing optimization for a deliberately limited set of residential properties.

The positioning is explicitly human-first: real people on guest communication, no bots, and quality over quantity in the size of the managed portfolio. Pricing is a flat 18% of gross bookings. At publication the portfolio holds a 4.92-star guest rating across 52 Airbnb reviews.

Jasper Properties has been a principal holding in the Common Ground portfolio since 2024, part of the group’s real estate practice alongside Lïef Development + Construction.
