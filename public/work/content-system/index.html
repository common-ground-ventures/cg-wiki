Content system: a posting plan for the group's brands that stayed mostly internal

Work · Operating · Jimmy Khounlavong

Khounlavong set the cadence, the channel rules and the templates for posting across the Common Ground brands. The system was built and largely did not publish.

Wiki/Work/Content system

What it is

Common Ground and its ventures each have a public channel. One person approves what goes out. The system had to let that one person run several brands on a repeating weekly rhythm.

This was internal work. It was not sold to a client. Most of what it produced was drafted and never posted, and this page says so in its results.

What Khounlavong did

  • Started a weekly rhythm on March 2, 2026, with a weekly pack of drafts for each brand.
  • Set the cadence on May 2, 2026 by how mature each brand was: three posts a week, Tuesday to Thursday, for Common Ground, Lïef Development, APEXX AI and Fluidmapper, and one or two a week for brands still before launch.
  • Set the production model: one carousel on Tuesday, and two single posts drawn from it on Wednesday and Thursday.
  • Set the channel rule for LinkedIn: principals post from their own accounts and the company page reshares.
  • Ruled that he does not speak as Common Ground on his own LinkedIn. His own page is anchored to Lip Loi Creative.
  • Limited Instagram to the brands where it fits.
  • Approved a Common Ground program for the second quarter of 2026: 13 weeks and 39 pieces, one carousel and two image posts a week.
  • Built carousel templates for Common Ground, APEXX AI and Lïef.
  • Owns the social media procedure of May 12, 2026, which sets out who originates, who reviews and where the work runs.

Results

  • The program was largely not published. A log of April 27, 2026 records that the three quarterly programs sat ready and none had been loaded for posting.
  • The scheduling tool last sent original content on March 17 to 19, 2026. One Common Ground reshare on July 13, 2026 was the only later activity recorded.
  • A weekly recap in late April 2026 recorded 32 impressions for the week.
  • Weekly packs of drafts were still produced through July 23, 2026.
  • The program's draft copy contains performance claims that no document supports. They are draft marketing copy, and none is repeated here.
