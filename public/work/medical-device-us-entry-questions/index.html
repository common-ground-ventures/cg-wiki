How did the exclusivity finding change the channel plan?

What stayed viable was introductions to specific buyers, not a full market launch. Entering the U.S. market takes real investment: new compliance, a new corporate structure and all new contracts, plus investor readiness work before any introduction lands. A commission-only arrangement does not cover that, so we never put full resources behind a launch. We still shop it, and it sits on the back burner for us. If a real buyer comes up, an assisted living operator for example, we would work to make that deal, and with a buyer in hand we would revisit the terms.
