Nike Marketplace Merchandising: four assignments in nine months

Work · Brand · Jimmy Khounlavong

Moved from Basketball to Finish Line, to City Specialty and boutiques, to Hibbett and Shoe Palace inside one fiscal year, and left a door segmentation or a three-year merchandising strategy behind in each seat.

Wiki/Work/Nike Marketplace Merchandising

What it is

Nike split merchandising into category merchandising and a new marketplace function. The channel received one Best and Better catalog no matter what a retailer stood for.

His own word for the year was evolution. "Over the course of 9 months, I have had 4 significant role and account base changes."

What Khounlavong did

  • Finish Line, second quarter: the first door segmentation with planning, a three-year merchandising strategy, and a store teardown in Indianapolis.
  • City Specialty and boutiques, third quarter: the City Specialty Assortment Rollout Strategy, 11 versions in five weeks, sorting accounts into six groups with a season-by-season handover from catalogs to built assortments.
  • Hibbett and Shoe Palace, fourth quarter: the door cluster framework, built on the retailer's own three consumer profiles, with a muse door for each cluster.
  • Took Nike's Create the Future merchandising reorganization to each account, rewriting the internal deck in the retailer's language.
  • Led the marketplace merchandising session at the city specialty summit in Las Vegas, February 2016.
  • Grew from one direct report to four, and wrote the job profiles for the merchants he hired.
  • Raised productivity and profitability 35% across three key retail partners.
  • Sped up the new functional process so that product landed 10 days before launch.
  • Told Hibbett, "You are three different companies," Fashion, Athletic and Sport, and built a cluster for each.
  • Held that the work was about getting a retailer to a more informed starting point, not dictating what to buy.

Results

  • The City Specialty strategy and the Door Cluster Framework are both recorded as completed in his review.
