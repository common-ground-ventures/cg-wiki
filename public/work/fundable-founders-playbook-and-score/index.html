The Fundable Founder's Playbook and Fundability Score: the studio's method, sold as a product

Work · Brand · Jimmy Khounlavong

Khounlavong turned Lip Loi Creative's two-phase method into a free scored assessment and a 73-page workbook, and his own file records no Playbook sales by April 17, 2026.

Wiki/Work/The Fundable Founder's Playbook and Fundability Score

What it is

Lip Loi Creative works in two phases. Phase 1, Market Reality, asks one question: does this business deserve to exist as it currently is? Phase 2, the Fundability Engine, asks whether the business can survive investor scrutiny, operationally, financially and narratively. The studio's site states the rule between them: validate before you narrate.

The Playbook names the problem it was written for: "Founders often confuse storytelling with fundability." The product ladder puts the method in a founder's hands at three levels. The first is a free assessment. The second is a self-guided workbook. The third is work with the studio.

Khounlavong wrote the Playbook and created its two phases, Market Reality and Fundability. He founded Lip Loi Creative. Jesse Fowler holds the studio's revenue seat and co-signed the Playbook.

What Khounlavong did

  • Built the first draft, dated January 28, 2026, as a four-step system of 27 pages. By January 31 it was a five-step system of 64 pages. The version on sale, v.3.1.D, is dated March 30, 2026 and runs 73 pages.
  • Organized the workbook in two phases and five modules: market validation, the strategic wedge, the narrative stack, operational physics and investor readiness.
  • Ended each module with a decision tool: a question, a forced choice or a checkpoint that tells the reader whether to move forward or go back. The instructions call it "a machine to run", not a book to read.
  • Credited the source frameworks by name on an attribution page, among them The Mom Test, Building a StoryBrand, Obviously Awesome and Crossing the Chasm. The opening note reads: "This playbook is not built on original theory. It's built on original exposure."
  • Built the worksheets into the document. They include the Gap Map, the Anti-Avatar Generator, the Villain Dossier, the Positioning Generator, the Pronoun Audit, the One-Page Pitch Skeleton, the Data Room Checklist and the Objection Response Matrix.
  • Added a bonus prompt that walks a founder through building a 12-month profit and loss statement with an AI assistant.
  • Built companion profit and loss workbooks in four versions between January 29 and February 3, 2026, and a financial framework workbook dated March 31, 2026.
  • Built the Fundability Score, a free assessment hosted on ScoreApp and embedded in the studio's site. The personalized report gives two scores, Brand Strategy and Fundability Strength, and places the founder in one of four archetypes: Blank Canvas, Hidden Gem, Hype Risk or Unicorn.
  • Tagged each assessment result by archetype in the studio's email list, so follow-up could match the result.
  • Rebuilt the studio's site from Framer to Next.js, starting March 24, 2026. The repository shows 30 commits through September 23, 2026. The site offers three ways in: a weekly newsletter, the Playbook and work with the studio.

Results

  • The assessment and the Playbook are live on the studio's site.
  • Khounlavong's own venture file, updated April 17, 2026, records the sales position in plain words: "Zero sales to date. Not a framework problem. An audience scale problem."
  • He made a decision on that evidence. The same file reframes the Playbook as a lead qualifier for consulting work, not a revenue driver at the studio's current audience size.
  • In his account of September 19, 2026, the ladder was in market and had not yet been promoted on social channels.
