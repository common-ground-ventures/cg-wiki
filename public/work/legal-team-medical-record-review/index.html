An in-house legal team: medical-record review at one-tenth the time

Work · Technology

Zen Aegis automated the gathering, summarizing and delivery of medical records for an in-house legal team; one case that historically cost about $75,000 came in at a fraction of the cost in one-tenth of the time.

Wiki/Work/Medical-record review for an in-house legal team

What it is

An in-house legal team was doing its medical-record work by hand. Paralegals gathered the records, read them, summarized them and put the result into a form an attorney could use, case after case, at a recurring cost in the hundreds of thousands.

The decision

Zen Aegis took the manual paralegal steps on as one automated workflow, from gathering the records to delivering a summary an attorney can work from,.

What Zen Aegis did

  • Automated the gathering of medical records for each case.
  • Automated the summarization of those records.
  • Automated delivery of attorney-ready work product, replacing the manual paralegal steps.
  • Built a separate medical-record review product as a proof of concept on synthetic data: it builds a chronology of significant events, surfaces contradictions and gaps, and keeps every assertion traceable to a source line.

Results

  • On one case, work that historically cost about $75,000 was delivered at a fraction of the cost in one-tenth of the time, a 90 percent cut in time.
  • Hundreds of thousands of dollars in recurring paralegal cost displaced.
