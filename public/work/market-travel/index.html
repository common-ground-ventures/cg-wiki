Market Travel: one format, six cities

Work · Operating · Jimmy Khounlavong

A repeatable way to bring product teams to the consumer: a mission, a door list by tier, focus groups, then a recap with owners and dates.

Wiki/Work/Market Travel

What it is

Six documented trips share one format across Basketball and Running.

What Khounlavong did

  • Las Vegas, July 2014: led the footwear section of focus groups with 17-year-old athletes.
  • Los Angeles, December 2014: 34 retail locations and a varsity team focus group.
  • Dallas, January 2015: footwear captain for a 15-person sporting goods trip.
  • Chicago, June 2015: 28 retail locations and two focus groups.
  • New York, December 2016: run crews and leading accounts, with four learnings and four ways to win.
  • Los Angeles, February 2017: hosted the team, with account meetings at Undefeated, BAIT and Nice Kicks, and a run with Koreatown Run Club.

Results

  • The Koreatown crew later appeared in the 2018 React by Run Crews project.
