Ritz-Carlton, Laguna Beach: an outdoor patio and branded-residence advisory

Work · Development

Two narrow Tellus-era credits: an outdoor patio built for the Ritz-Carlton in Laguna Beach and development advisory on Ritz-Carlton branded residences.

Wiki/Work/Ritz-Carlton Laguna Beach

What it is

Two separate, narrow credits from the Tellus Design and Build years, both Fowler's. The first is construction: an outdoor patio built for the Ritz-Carlton at its Laguna Beach property in California. The second is advisory: development advisory on luxury branded residences carrying the Ritz-Carlton name.

What Common Ground did

  • Tellus Design and Build built an outdoor patio for the Ritz-Carlton at its Laguna Beach property, delivered to a luxury operator's standard.
  • Separately, Fowler provided development advisory on luxury branded residences carrying the Ritz-Carlton name: the questions a branded residential product raises for the operator that owns the brand and the land.
  • The approach that runs through both: on any engagement that touches a client's real property, ask whether the current use has been compared recently against its next-best use. A contractor doing the physical work is often the one party standing in both the facilities meeting and the capital conversation, so the larger question gets raised once, to whoever holds the authority to hear it, and then the contracted work gets finished.
  • Asking the bigger question has a cost, since a client who wanted a patio can read it as overreach. Fowler raises it once, to the right seat, and then finishes the work he was hired for. Two accurate, narrow credits beat one inflated one: Tellus built a patio for the Ritz-Carlton, it did not build the Ritz-Carlton.
