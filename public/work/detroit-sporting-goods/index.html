Midwest Sporting Goods retailers: mutual stock-to-sales plans with two chains

Work · Operating · Jimmy Khounlavong

Eight months into Nike, took the lead seat on MC Sports and Dunham's, two of the Central territory's largest sporting goods accounts, and built mutual stock-to-sales plans with both, so the seasonal buys came from a plan the retailer and Nike had made together.

Wiki/Work/Midwest Sporting Goods retailers

What it is

His first-year goals were to learn the two accounts, form a view on Cleveland and Detroit, and learn retail math well enough to make the retailer more profitable.

What Khounlavong did

  • Pioneered a shared stock to sales plan with MC Sports and pushed it into common practice.
  • Built an anniversary form, a category planning tool and a football planning tool with a user guide for other reps.
  • Set a weekly and monthly information schedule with MC Sports, and one-page performance recaps for both accounts.
  • Moved both chains onto electronic ordering.
  • Wrote three-year strategic plans for both accounts. The goal for one: "Make shopping at MC Sports cool."
  • Answered a request for Under Armour intelligence in July 2006 with share and sell-through by model from the shelf.

Results

  • Fiscal 2005 account base up 5% to $13.7M. Fiscal 2007 up 8%.
  • MC Sports, 2006: sales up 18%, gross margin up 22%.
  • Football cleat share at Dunham's reached 65% in fiscal 2009.
  • Grew 1% in fiscal 2009, in a down market.
  • MC Sports Footwear Vendor and Rep of the Year, 2006.
