Three client builds: a strategy firm, a youth sports nonprofit and a family foundation

Work · Technology

Three live client builds: a rebuilt homepage and development standard for a strategy consulting firm, a site with online registration for a youth sports nonprofit, and a hosted site for a family foundation.

Wiki/Work/Sites for a strategy firm, a youth sports nonprofit and a family foundation

What it is

Three smaller clients each needed a site that worked and kept working. A strategy consulting firm needed its homepage and offer rebuilt. A youth sports nonprofit needed a site that could take registrations. A family foundation needed a site up, hosted and on its own domain.

The decision

For the strategy firm, Zen Aegis wrote a development standard and an element registry along with the pages, so later pages follow the same rules instead of drifting.

For the nonprofit, registrations run through a webhook, with form alerts to the team.

What Zen Aegis did

  • Strategy consulting firm: rebuilt the homepage through four versions, wrote the full site copy, wireframes and secondary pages, set a development standard and element registry, ran a production-readiness audit and defined the service packages.
  • Youth sports nonprofit: built the website and hosting, form alerts and a registration webhook, across 15 milestones.
  • Family foundation: built a hosted site of three to five pages and configured its domain.

Results

  • All three builds are live.
  • The nonprofit's registration system shipped across 15 milestones.
