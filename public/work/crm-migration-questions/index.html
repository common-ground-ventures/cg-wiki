CRM Migration: questions answered

Work · Brand

Common Ground on CRM Migration.

Wiki/Work/CRM Migration/Questions answered

Questions about CRM Migration, answered in the words of the people who did the work. The engagement itself is on its own page.

What changed in the team's daily behavior once the CRM was live?

Honestly, the team does not do anything differently. They do less. We built custom agents with their own skills for CRM tracking and uploading, and they read every email, text message and outreach touch and keep updating the CRM as things happen. That is what keeps it current. Adoption stopped depending on a salesperson doing their own tracking, which is the line that historically always gets missed. Once the tracking is agentic, keeping a CRM current becomes easy, and for the first time the process is bulletproof.

What safeguards keep the agents from writing bad information into the CRM?

It is constantly evolving. You do not get it right the first time. Things come up in the real world, and the agents get iterated every time they do. The first safeguard is what never gets written at all. Anything personal and anything legal stays out. Calls are recorded, and sometimes a recorded call is not a business call, so those have to be safeguarded before anything touches a record. The CRM holds the business relationship, not the person's private life.
