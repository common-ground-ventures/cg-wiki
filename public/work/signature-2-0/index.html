Signature 2.0: resetting a business that had outrun its consumer

Work · Brand · Jimmy Khounlavong

After four years of growth, signature basketball sell-through was slowing as prices rose. He wrote the five-step reset, and a companion plan for everything under $120.

Wiki/Work/Signature 2.0

What it is

Fiscal 2015 was a record, and the first year past $500M. His own review that year: "The marketplace has shifted greatly in the past year and Nike Basketball's footwear growth trajectory has slowed."

The diagnosis on the slide: as prices increased and overlapped, signature sell-through slowed and the category vacated critical price points.

What Khounlavong did

  • Pioneered the Signature 2.0 reset plan: right-size the business, re-ignite energy, lead with product construct, diversify, and manage the portfolio as one.
  • Led Conquer the Core, the strategy for all footwear under $120, aimed at the high school athlete who wants visible innovation at great value.
  • Wrote the sporting goods strategy after a 15-person market trip to Dallas, where he was footwear captain.
  • Created the new three-year footwear strategy for the category.
  • Proposed cancelling 30% of KD 8 orders to restore a pull market, and cutting Elite allocations 20%.
