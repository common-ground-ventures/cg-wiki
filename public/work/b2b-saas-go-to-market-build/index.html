A B2B SaaS company: a full go-to-market build in five months

Work · Technology

Positioning, a new site, a blog system, four phases of search work, outreach and paid traffic for a B2B SaaS company, run as one retainer: 6,048 sessions in 130 days.

Wiki/Work/Go-to-market build for a B2B SaaS company

What it is

A B2B software company needed its whole go-to-market built: what it stands for, the site that says it, the content that keeps it found and the outreach and paid traffic that bring people in.

The decision

Zen Aegis took the whole go-to-market on as one retainer, positioning through paid traffic, and ran the pieces together for five months.

What Zen Aegis did

  • Wrote the positioning.
  • Built the website and a database-backed blog system.
  • Ran search engine optimization in four phases.
  • Built a LinkedIn content program and more than 15 outreach sequences.
  • Ran Google Ads, set the Reddit Ads strategy and built the post-trial nurture.

Results

  • 6,048 sessions and 5,572 users in 130 days, at a 75 percent engagement rate.
  • Paid traffic engaged at 99.4 percent across 2,557 sessions.
  • Peak month: 3,228 sessions, up 139 percent on the month before.
