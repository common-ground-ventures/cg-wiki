Nike By You: a rebrand executed mid-flight

Work · Brand · Jimmy Khounlavong

Co-authored the rebrand of NIKEiD to Nike By You and executed the reset of the line behind it, while the seasonal line kept shipping on calendar.

Wiki/Work/Nike By You

What it is

After 18 years, NIKEiD was a beloved utility but not a business. Customization sat outside Nike's category offense, carried aging models and spoke to a narrow audience.

The platform needed a new name, a new position and a new commercial engine, all while the seasonal machine kept shipping.

What Khounlavong did

  • Co-authored the rebrand strategy and contributed the consumer insight and the product and materials demand data that aimed it at the right consumer.
  • Executed the reset of the line from always-on extensions to fewer, better, tiered product, and set a three-tier pricing framework in October 2017.
  • Rebuilt the commercial model: new digital merchandising and product lifecycle models, a global launch calendar, and financial tooling that connected creative decisions to margin.
  • Launched co-created product with Patta, Bodega, Pendleton and Kendrick Lamar, set the strategy for the Nigel Sylvester Air Force 1 that launched after his move to Los Angeles, and built the fiscal 2019 pipeline with CLOT and Heron Preston.
  • Brought the ACG x Pendleton x Jay Gordon Mowabb to ComplexCon and Bodega Boston, on a calendar of about four months from concept to launch.
  • Wired customization into the category line plans and the Nike membership offering, ending its status as a siloed product.
  • Launched and merchandised the Nike member shop.
  • Built the tools the function had never had: a financial comparison tool, a global broadcast deck, a visual launch calendar and a seasonal concept map.
  • Merchandised the All the Stars of LA Air Force 1 for NBA All-Star 2018.
  • Drove sole-swap innovation on the Heron Preston Air Max 720/95.
  • Created the Cultivator influencer financial model, so that seeding and partnership ran as a measured commercial program.
  • Started a monthly cross-functional Lunch and Learn series to develop future leaders.
  • Worked back through factories, warehouses and outside partners on custom repackaging, numbered pairs and special deliveries.
  • Led the Membership Programming and iD Integration workstream inside Nike Direct merchandising.
  • Built the Pendleton material buy by yardage and minimums, the mechanism behind the materials reduction.
  • Built the fall 2019 line plan framework, a planning tool with targets by tier.
  • Extended the pipeline to Pensole, Maharishi and Swarovski.

Results

  • Grew the platform to $181M, delivering 30% fiscal 2018 global revenue growth across Nike By You and the Nike.com and direct integration it powered.
  • Excess materials cut 34%, a $2M reduction.
  • One brand, unified from a legacy platform.
  • Member shop: $5.4M in demand, 22,200 units, 46% sell-through.
  • Beat budget by 15% on the year.
  • Project demand in fiscal 2018: Born Mercurial $1.7M, NBA by Air Force 1 $1.3M, NBA Hyperlocal City $558K, Epic React by Run Crews $304K, Air Max Day $216K, the Mowabb $149K, Patta $96K.
  • 7.3M product page visits through the Nike+ membership offering.
  • The winter premium pack with Pendleton wools produced $2.5M and 19,400 units, 188% over budget.
  • The Metcon 4 launch produced $1.1M and 8,000 units in its first month, 194% over budget.
  • The Pegasus Shield produced $1M and 6,900 units.
  • The Kobe jersey retirement Air Force 1 sold about 1,000 units and sold out overnight.
  • Holiday delivery carried 83,964 guaranteed orders, with 4 late.
