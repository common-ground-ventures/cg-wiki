Midwest Boutiques: open, invest, divest, harvest

Work · Brand · Jimmy Khounlavong

Re-mapped Nike Sportswear across Chicago, Detroit, Minneapolis, Milwaukee and Cleveland, door by door, and gave each door a level of distribution it had earned.

Wiki/Work/Midwest Boutiques

What it is

More than 25 boutique and urban accounts across the upper Midwest, from St. Alfred and Leaders to Burn Rubber and Succezz.

What Khounlavong did

  • Sorted every door into invest, divest or harvest, and set a distribution level for each.
  • Opened Phenom in Minneapolis and Pogo outside Detroit, and started Haberdash in Chicago to reach a 25 to 34 consumer.
  • Lowered distribution at doors that were not moving the market, and withheld Air Force 1 from a door with poor presentation.
  • Stabilized the boutiques by adding inline performance product beside Nike Sportswear.
  • Wrote strategic plans for four accounts and held quarterly trunk shows.

Results

  • Fall and holiday 2010 bookings in those doors: basketball up 77.9%, running up 418.9%, training up 127.4%, with the urban and indie business held.
