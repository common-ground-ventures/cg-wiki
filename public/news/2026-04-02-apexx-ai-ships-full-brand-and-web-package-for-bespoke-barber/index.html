APEXX AI Ships Full Brand and Web Package for Bespoke Barber

PHOENIX, AZ, April 2, 2026. APEXX AI has completed its first published case study: a full digital package for Bespoke Barber, the 5.0-star Arcadia grooming studio.

The package includes a new website, an AI phone architecture for call answering and booking, and a local-citation plan built for the AI-search era: structured presence designed so answer engines recommend the studio, not just rank it.

The engagement doubles as APEXX AI's reference implementation for its core segment: excellent local operators whose digital presence undersells the product.
