Common Ground Contracted to Build Kinetic ML, a Lead-Intelligence Consolidation Proof of Concept

News · July 1, 2026

Posted September 2026. Covers July 1, 2026.

Posted September 2026 · covers July 1, 2026

PHOENIX, AZ, July 1, 2026. Common Ground has been contracted to create Kinetic ML, an exploratory proof of concept that consolidates the fragmented lead-generation stack, the research, enrichment, and outreach tooling sold as a half-dozen separate subscriptions, into a single pipeline.

The live demo at kineticml.com runs the full sequence in one pass: it scrapes a target business's site, pulls reviews, finds social profiles, identifies the decision maker through a seven-source discovery waterfall, generates candidate email addresses across nine confidence-scored patterns, and writes personalized outreach, streaming each step to the user in real time. All inputs are public records and public web data, processed on demand.

The product carries the portfolio's standard credits: branded by Lip Loi Creative, enhanced by APEXX AI.
