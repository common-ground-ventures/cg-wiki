Common Ground Ships Cascade IR: Investor Materials That Refresh in Place with Zero Formatting Drift

News · July 8, 2026

Posted September 2026. Covers July 8, 2026.

Posted September 2026 · covers July 8, 2026

PHOENIX, AZ, July 8, 2026. Common Ground has shipped Cascade IR, a proof-of-concept engine that solves an unglamorous problem every raising company has: keeping approved investor decks and marketing materials current without breaking them.

Cascade refreshes approved materials in place. Numbers, dates, and narrative blocks update from source data while layout, typography, and brand treatment stay byte-stable, zero formatting drift, no "final_v7_REALLY_FINAL" folder. The engine is in production and demonstrable live.

The insight is that investor materials are not documents, they are interfaces to a data set that changes weekly. Cascade treats them that way.
