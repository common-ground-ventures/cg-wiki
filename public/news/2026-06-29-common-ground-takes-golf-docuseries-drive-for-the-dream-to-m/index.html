Common Ground Takes Golf Docuseries "Drive for the Dream" to Market

News · June 29, 2026

Posted September 2026. Covers June 29, 2026.

Posted September 2026 · covers June 29, 2026

PHOENIX, AZ, June 29, 2026. Common Ground has taken Drive for the Dream, its golf docuseries property, to market with a sports-media distribution strategy and a completed investor package.

The series follows professional golfers grinding on the game's developmental edge, and the talent bench is signed: seven players are attached, including Jhared Hack, the 2007 Western Amateur champion who beat Dustin Johnson and Rickie Fowler in match play on his way to the title. The property goes out with a full pitch deck, a nine-section investor room covering opportunity, model, traction, and team, and a distribution strategy aimed at the sports-business media ecosystem.

Golf's audience economics have never been better, and the underdog format is the one lane the incumbents keep leaving open.
