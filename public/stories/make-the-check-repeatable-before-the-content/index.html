Wiki/Stories/Make the check repeatable before the content

Our first prospect data room was built decision by decision: folders, access, layout, write-up style. It worked, and it couldn't be repeated.

We turned it into one template, one access standard and one layout, with a rule that nothing goes live until it's checked against the room and its own sources.

A room that looks live and isn't is worse than no room. The check is what catches it before a client does.

That pattern let the team stand up seven private rooms in parallel in one day, in June 2026.

Standardize the verification first. The content follows.
