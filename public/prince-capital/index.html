Wiki/Prince Capital

Overview

Prince Capital is a private investment advisory firm founded by Alex Prince, Common Ground’s Capital & Investment principal and CFO.

Within the Common Ground ecosystem, capital work sits under Prince Capital. Advisory and operating work stays with the group; securities are offered through Finalis Securities LLC, Member FINRA/SIPC, a separate and unaffiliated firm. Prince Capital is not a registered broker-dealer. Prince Capital has been a principal holding in the portfolio since 2023.

Prince Capital is a sister company of Common Ground; Jesse Fowler serves as its fractional CRO. The firm’s own engagements, deal stories and Alex Prince’s career are written up on the Prince Capital wiki.
