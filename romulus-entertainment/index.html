Wiki/Romulus Entertainment

Overview

Romulus Entertainment is a film production company that develops and produces feature films across genres. Its portfolio lists ten films, including Georgetown (Christoph Waltz, Annette Bening), American Woman (Sienna Miller, Aaron Paul), City of Lies (Johnny Depp, Forest Whitaker), The Banker (Anthony Mackie, Samuel L. Jackson), Driven, Dreamland, Monster, Gully, Jungleland, and Waldo.

Common Ground’s relationship with Romulus is advisory and dates to 2016, the oldest relationship in the portfolio. Alex Prince, the group’s Capital & Investment principal, is co-GP of the Romulus Entertainment Fund.
