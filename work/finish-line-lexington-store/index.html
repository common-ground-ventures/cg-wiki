The Finish Line: a store grown from $3M to $5M

Work · Operating · Jimmy Khounlavong

Khounlavong started at The Finish Line at 16, became an assistant manager in Lexington, and helped grow annual store volume from $3M to $5M.

Wiki/Work/The Finish Line

What it is

Khounlavong grew up in Ashland, Kentucky. He went to work at The Finish Line, a mall based athletic footwear retailer, at the age of 16, and stayed until he graduated from the University of Kentucky in 1999. He has described the start as the stock room of his local sneaker store.

He held two roles at two stores. He was a sales associate in Ashland from 1993 to 1997, and an assistant manager in Lexington from 1997 to 1999.

What Khounlavong did

  • Managed a mall based sales team as assistant manager. His 2015 profile records a team of more than 15.
  • Delivered product presentations to the associates so that product knowledge was retained.
  • Created seasonal floor plans to match the seasonal visual merchandising strategies.
  • Served as District Product Information Specialist and as a sales associate trainer.
  • Led the Sports Moments traveling sales team.
  • Partnered with third party vendors to launch retail sales programs, and developed training programs and protocol.

Results

  • Annual store volume grew from $3M to $5M.
  • His 2007 resume gives a second figure for a small format location: $3.2M in annual sales.
