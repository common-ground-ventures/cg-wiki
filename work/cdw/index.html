CDW: 125 accounts and a $15M book in Chicago

Work · Operating · Jimmy Khounlavong

Between Medline and Nike, Khounlavong sold technology for CDW in Chicago, carrying 125 accounts and more than $15M in revenue, up 5% on the prior year.

Wiki/Work/CDW

What it is

CDW is a technology reseller headquartered outside Chicago. Khounlavong joined in 2002, after three years selling medical supplies for Medline in Memphis, and left in March 2004 for Nike. The seat was account sales: a book of 125 accounts, each with its own buying cycle, and a revenue number to beat.

It was the last stop before Nike and the one that moved him to Chicago, where his Nike career began the same year.

What Khounlavong did

  • Carried a book of 125 accounts for CDW in Chicago from 2002 to 2004.
  • Managed more than $15M in revenue across those accounts.
  • Grew the book 5% over the prior year.
  • Left in March 2004 to join Nike as a marketplace footwear sales representative in Chicago, covering more than 200 accounts across five states.

Results

  • More than $15M in revenue managed, up 5% on the prior year.
  • The move to Chicago that put him in Nike's Central territory in March 2004.
