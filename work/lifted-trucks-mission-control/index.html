Lifted Trucks: a mission-control prototype for multi-state inventory

Work · Technology

A collective Common Ground proof of concept for Lifted Trucks, a prospective client: a working six-screen demo of what a mission control for multi-state inventory could look like, shipped May 21, 2026.

Wiki/Work/Lifted Trucks mission control

What it is

Lifted Trucks sells across several states, and the question was what a single mission control for its inventory could look like. This is capability work for a prospective client, not a delivered engagement.

The decision

Common Ground answered the question with a working demo the prospect could click through, built on the concept the group had led.

What was built

  • Built a six-screen, AI-native inventory intelligence prototype across several states.
  • Shipped the working demo on May 21, 2026, with a walkthrough of twelve screenshots.

Results

  • A working demo shipped May 21, 2026 and still running in October 2026.
