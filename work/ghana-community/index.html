Ghana, 1,000 acres: a master plan built as an economy before a drawing

Work · Development

Master plan and schematic design proposal for a new community in Ghana's Volta Region, with the building system and local crew training in scope.

Wiki/Work/Ghana Community

What it is

A developer brought LÏEF Development a program for a new community on about 1,000 acres in Ghana's Volta Region, about an hour and a half east of Accra against the Akwapim-Togo range. The idea had started as a taxi-union app with EV charging, grown into an EV factory with battery plants, and become a city as the land grew.

The program read like the contents of a town: 1,500 detached homes, a 100-home luxury enclave, four EV factories, a hospital, a university, a stadium, civic buildings and markets. The request was for schematic design. The site floods in the rainy season, sits on laterite soils with termites, and depends on a local grid the developer did not trust.

Ghana has seen both kinds of plan. Hope City was launched near Accra in 2013 on the strength of a striking rendering and was never built. Akosombo Township, planned with the dam in the 1960s around a real economic anchor, still functions in the same hills.

What Common Ground did

  • The whole Common Ground team worked it through LÏEF. Jesse Fowler sat as principal, testing every design decision against constructability, budget, phasing and the pro forma, and carrying the bidding and construction phases.
  • Treated the request as a development problem first. On the kickoff call the developer's team put local wages at around $7,000 a year for an accountant, while the top homes in Accra sell for up to $3 million. The people buying most of the standard homes cannot pay what the enclave buyers can, so the plan had to say where the money for roads, water, power and treatment comes from, and who builds the homes, before what the city looks like.
  • Turned the program list into a site structure: six development zones on a grid, A through F, a preliminary land-use allocation across twelve uses, a street hierarchy of arterial, collector and local roads, utility corridors, green-belt separation and cycle and pedestrian links. The Phase 1 scope named the deliverables a client can hold a firm to: the master site plan, enlarged zone plans, massing studies, prototype floor plans for the standard and luxury homes, density and parking analysis, the open-space plan, infrastructure corridors and a design narrative fit for investors and regulators.
  • Recommended SABS, a three-layer composite panel system with no organic material for termites to eat and no host for mold, carrying wind and seismic ratings and cutting cooling load under an equatorial sun. Fowler knew how it behaves on a job from running the bid desk on SABS panel work in the Xtrata seat, and knew from leading the Greensburg, Kansas rebuild that a place gets built when someone has solved who pays for it and who builds it. On-site training for local crews in fabrication, installation and finish, with quality oversight through construction, went inside the design scope, so the build leaves trained trades behind instead of imported crews who leave nothing.
  • Closed the proposal with fifteen questions the plan could not be drawn honestly without, from site shape and soils to income profiles by housing type, energy strategy, cooking fuel, governing codes, the stadium's size and the security and governance model.
  • When the kickoff call surfaced the developer's own architect, the flood risk and a concern about paying a large share of the fee before the collaboration had proven itself, re-scoped the role to the seats nobody else held: master planner, storyteller and buildable-system layer beside him, with the fee split into smaller milestones tied to deliverables. The call also added cycle lanes, bus rapid transit on the model of Bogotá's TransMilenio, a solar field, on-site water and waste treatment and possibly an on-site panel or brick factory, which widened the training offer from installation to fabrication.
  • Delivered a concept direction two days after the call, rooted in the place: Accra's colonial, independence-era and contemporary layers and the Volta's Ewe vernacular. It set an eight-color palette drawn from local sources (Laterite Red, Kaolin White, West African Indigo, Kente Gold, Adinkra Charcoal, Volta Verdigris, Harmattan Ochre, Ewe Earth Brown), Adinkra symbols as wayfinding, a transit spine from industry through the civic core to the homes, and a four-part story for the city: a real economic anchor, one city for everyone in it, rooted in climate and culture, built by Ghanaian hands.
  • Wrote a finish playbook so the panels read as Ghanaian rather than imported: hand-trowelled lime plaster in laterite red for civic buildings and kaolin white for homes, a laterite block plinth about 600 mm high against rainy-season splash, breeze-block screens on west walls, zinc roofs floated above the shell as parasols, deep east and west overhangs, indigo doors and solar on every roof. It named the failure modes to engineer around: never let the foam core see sun, schedule panel work for the dry season, raise plinths on flood-prone lots and detail joints against termites.
  • Modeled the claim the story leans on, that the enclave helps carry the homes around it. Only the high enclave case against the low infrastructure case closes; everywhere else the plan needs a source of money it has not yet named. The line is a hypothesis, and the fifteen questions exist to replace the modeled inputs with real ones.

Results

  • A Phase 1 proposal covering the full site, with the building system and a local workforce plan inside the design scope.
  • A concept direction delivered to the developer two days after the kickoff call.
  • The proposal is unsigned while the developer raises capital for the project.
  • The lesson Fowler carries forward: the concept direction would have made a better first step than whole-site schematic design with half the fee up front, so on the next master-plan pursuit the concept direction is the first paid step and the schematic proposal follows it.
