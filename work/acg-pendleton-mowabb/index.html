ACG by Pendleton and the Mowabb: seed at the boutique, scale on Nike.com

Work · Brand · Jimmy Khounlavong

A customization launch run as a collaboration: two physical pairs by collector Jay Gordon at Bodega and ComplexCon, then the customization on Nike.com.

Wiki/Work/ACG by Pendleton and the Mowabb

What it is

Customization had always been open-ended. This was the first time the platform curated a small, select set.

What Khounlavong did

  • Managed the Mowabb from inception to launch.
  • Ran a ten-step process from July 17 to November 9, 2017.
  • Launched physical pairs at the Bodega door in Boston and the Bodega booth at ComplexCon, ahead of Nike.com.

Results

  • $149K in demand on a four-style assortment.
  • 300 finished pairs of the Mowabb sold out in hours. Pendleton product came to about 1,000 units in total.
