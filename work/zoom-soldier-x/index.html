The Zoom Soldier: a shoe the athlete can be proud to wear, at any income

Work · Brand · Jimmy Khounlavong

Held LeBron's accessible tier at $125 to $130 under a $200 game shoe, and re-imagined the franchise for its tenth year around his true fans.

Wiki/Work/The Zoom Soldier

What it is

The statement LeBron sat at $180 to $220. The challenge was a shoe the athlete could be proud to wear regardless of income.

What Khounlavong did

  • Held the Soldier as the tiered LeBron shoe, inline, as energy and through team basketball.
  • Extended it toward a four-season, eleven-month proposition across owned retail and the major chains.
  • Set the brief and the price position for the Zoom Soldier X before leaving Basketball in September 2015.
  • The brief: re-imagine the franchise for its tenth year and celebrate LeBron's true fans.

Results

  • Zoom Soldier X: $130 price point, 80% sell-through in six months.
  • "The shoe proved to be the most successful model in franchise history."
