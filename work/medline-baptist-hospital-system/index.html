Medline: contract adoption and a pain management plan across 18 hospitals

Work · Operating · Jimmy Khounlavong

Before Nike, Khounlavong sold medical supplies to an 18-facility hospital system from Memphis, and produced two separate results: $20M through contract adoption and about 11% growth through a pain management plan.

Wiki/Work/Medline

What it is

Khounlavong went into medical sales straight out of the University of Kentucky. He moved to Memphis, Tennessee as a medical supply sales representative for Medline Industries. His account was the Baptist Hospital System in the Mid-South, an organization of 18 facilities.

The selling was contract selling. Proposals ran for multiple years and multiple millions of dollars, and went to surgeons, nurses and hospital executives.

What Khounlavong did

  • Developed and presented multi-year contract proposals to the 18-facility system.
  • Administered a $30M roll-out of surgical packs, plastics and wound care.
  • Identified opportunities within a surgery pack offering that lowered the wholesale price to the hospital by $120 each and raised Medline's profit by $180 each.
  • Created and executed an "attack plan" for pain management trays for fiscal 2001.
  • Prepared and delivered product education presentations to nurses, physicians, surgeons and director level staff.
  • Built account specific sales forecasts and goals.

Results

  • Revenue growth of $20M through contract adoption.
  • Sales growth of 10.5%, or $2.0M, from the pain management plan in fiscal 2001. His later profiles round the figure to 11%.
  • 110% of his fiscal 2000 sales goal.
  • 1999 Rookie Sales Rep of the Year, Medline, as listed on his 2019 resumes.
