The pitch for Los Angeles, June 2018: a plan written before the job

Work · Brand · Jimmy Khounlavong

This page covers a pitch. Khounlavong wrote it in June 2018 as a candidate for the Los Angeles role, before he held it, and nothing in it is a delivered result.

Wiki/Work/The pitch for Los Angeles, June 2018

What it is

In June 2018 Khounlavong was Senior Merchandising Director for Nike By You. Nike was filling a senior director seat in Los Angeles built around one consumer, the sneaker obsessed. He interviewed with a 17 page deck dated June 29, 2018. Three versions of it survive.

The deck had two halves. The first was his story and his record. The second was a consumer growth strategy for Los Angeles across fiscal 2019 to 2021. Every figure and step in that second half was a proposal.

What Khounlavong did

  • Wrote the plan statement. Consumer: the sneaker obsessed. Mission: unleash the potential of Los Angeles through sport. Position: One L.A.
  • Proposed three priorities. Engage: know Los Angeles to serve Los Angeles. Enable: access in must-win doors. Empower: new experiences.
  • Proposed a three year concept map: foundation in fiscal 2019, launch in fiscal 2020, scale in fiscal 2021. It carried a proposed revenue ladder of $1.1B, $1.3B and $1.5B.
  • Proposed a consumer council to gather product insight, and a regular product meeting with headquarters to build a shared line plan.
  • Proposed mapping the key growth zones in the market, driving depth in must-win doors and maximizing efficiencies in strategic doors.
  • Proposed a revised influencer seeding strategy, ending in a shared revenue model with influencers in fiscal 2021.
  • Described his method in four steps: build an executable framework, drive launch and flow, hold financial accountability, and turn strategy into operations.
  • Showed the ACG x Pendleton x Jay Gordon project as his process, a ten step timeline from July 17 to November 9.
  • Closed on a vision in three words: Make. Lives. Better.

Results

  • He took the seat. His fiscal 2019 year-end review shows his title changing from Senior Merchandising Director, Nike By You, in the first quarter to the senior Los Angeles role for the sneaker obsessed in the second.
  • Two ideas from the pitch appear in his later Los Angeles work. Must-win doors became a task in his February 2019 workshop and a list of 37 top destinations in his October 2019 plan. Planning by consumer mindset ran through both.
