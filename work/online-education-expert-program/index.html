An online education company: a complete expert program in month one

Work · Technology

A complete expert program for an online education company, 17 modules and more than 80 lessons on a 10-week path, delivered live inside the first month.

Wiki/Work/Expert program for an online education company

What it is

An online education company needed an expert program it could put in front of learners: a full path from foundations to capstone, with career tracks and a way to pilot it with a first cohort.

The decision

Zen Aegis priced the whole program as one fixed fee and committed to delivering it inside the first month.

What Zen Aegis did

  • Designed 17 modules and more than 80 lessons.
  • Laid out a 10-week learning path with three career tracks and a foundations module.
  • Wrote three capstone specifications.
  • Designed the pilot cohort.

Results

  • Delivered live inside month one.
  • The program is the basis of Zen Aegis's AI enablement and training work.
